### Artificial Intelligence / 人工智能
#### Model Capability Evaluation / 模型能力评测

**Evaluating Local Language Model Deployments: Answer Quality, Deliverable Completion, and Runtime**  
**本地语言模型部署评测：答卷质量、交付完成度与运行耗时**

1104 study: blind review, deliverable evidence, and end-to-end runtime across 12 local deployments and four fixed tasks.

Two human reviewers each completed all 48 cases. Their per-dimension scores correlate at Spearman ρ = 0.73, the pooled human ranking correlates with the model-judge ranking at ρ = 0.90, and the human totals sit 17–29 points below the model-judge totals on the 100-point scale.  
1104 研究中两位人工评审各完成全部 48 例：逐维评分的 Spearman 相关为 0.73，人工合并名次与模型评审名次的相关为 0.90，人工四题总分比模型评审低 17–29 分（百分制）。
